About Team Score

Team Score was built for weekend foursomes who want their whole group's game tracked, not just one card at a time.

Every group plays the game a little differently — Net, Gross, Stableford, Points Quota, Nines, Baseball, Six Six Six, or ABCD. Team Score lets a foursome set that up before the first tee shot, then keeps live team and individual scores for the round as it's played.
