Score the whole foursome, not just yourself.

Team Score tracks live team and individual results for your predetermined golf game — set it up before you tee off, then follow the scoring hole by hole.

See the game formats

Built for weekend foursomes

Before anyone tees off, your group sets up the game. Team Score then keeps the current round's team and individual scores as you play, against whatever format you picked.

Configurable courses

Save any course with per-hole par and handicap index, ready to reuse round after round.

Player handicaps built in

Add each player with their handicap so net scoring and points calculate automatically.

Live team scorecard

Follow running team totals hole by hole, with a toggle between Gross and Net display.

Full round summary

See every format's result for the round side by side — plus eagles, birdies, and pars per player.

Eight game formats, one app

Pick your format before you tee off — Team Score handles the scoring for all of them.

Net

Scores adjusted by handicap strokes for a level playing field.

Gross

Straight, unadjusted scoring hole by hole.

Stableford

Points awarded per hole based on score relative to par.

Points Quota

Track each player's points against their personal quota.

Nines

Points split among the group on every hole.

Baseball

Baseball-style scoring, available in both Net and Gross variants.

Six Six Six

Rotating partners and formats across three six-hole segments.

ABCD Game

Team scoring built around player rankings A through D.

How a round works

1

Set up the course

Pick a saved course or configure a new one with par and handicap for every hole.

2

Add your players

Enter each player's name and handicap before the round begins.

3

Enter scores as you go

Tap in scores hole by hole and follow the live team scorecard.

4

Review the summary

See results across every format for the round, plus per-player stats.
